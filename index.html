09 / FAQ

Questions before we build?

Clear answers to the things clients usually want to know before starting.

Do I need to provide content?

You can provide existing content, or WingBait can help structure and refine what you already have so the page communicates clearly.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild the experience around clearer messaging, stronger structure and better conversion paths.

How fast can you deliver?

Landing Page: 2–3 days. Website Sprint: 3–5 days. Website + Lead Automation: 5–7 days, depending on scope and integrations.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

Yes. WingBait can deploy the website and help configure hosting and domain settings.

Can you add WhatsApp or booking?

Yes. WhatsApp, click-to-call, enquiry forms and appointment booking can be connected where appropriate.

What exactly is lead automation?

It connects the website to systems that handle leads after an enquiry — notifications, CRM updates, follow-ups, qualification and booking workflows.

Is AI included?

AI can be used for selected tasks such as lead qualification, information extraction and workflow assistance, depending on the project.

Can I start small?

Yes. Start with a Landing Page and upgrade later as your website and lead system needs grow.
